Railway Construction and Violence against Women: Evidence from Mexico's Tren Maya

with Adán Silveiro Murillo

Using staggered difference-in-differences on hospital injury, crime, and formal employment records across treated municipalities in the Yucatán Peninsula, this paper identifies the effect of large infrastructure construction on intimate partner violence and non-partner female assault.
